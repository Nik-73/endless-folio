Take your specimen elsewhere

This experimental bookmarklet drops your specimen onto whatever page you are reading. It walks the text, lights up words, ties them together with thread and now and then shakes a paragraph loose. Press Escape or “Remove crawler” to put the page back.

On a laptop

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar, then click it on any article:

❦ Folio crawler

On a phone

  1. Copy the code below.
  2. Bookmark any page, then edit that bookmark.
  3. Replace its address with the copied code and name it “Folio crawler”.
  4. On the page you want, open your bookmarks and tap it (in Chrome, type the name in the address bar and pick the bookmark).

Try it on a sample article

Some sites forbid outside scripts and will block the crawler; it never sees passwords or forms and makes no network requests of its own. Text you read stays on your device.
